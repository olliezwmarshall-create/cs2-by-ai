The BRM files contain converted geometry and resized base-color images. UVs
are preserved; normal, emissive and metal/roughness maps are not used by this
renderer. Exact URLs, source hashes and conversion hashes are in sources.json.
The converter is tools/convert_gltf.py. No third-party code was imported.

All other geometry, character animation and procedural surface materials are
original work created for this project. These imported props improve scene
art; they do not replace the full renderer or constitute photorealistic art.
